🌙 About the Moon

The Moon is Earth's natural satellite. It is the fifth-largest natural satellite in the Solar System.

🌊 Tides

The Moon's gravity plays an important role in Earth's ocean tides.

🌍 Earth & Moon

The Moon takes about 27 days to orbit Earth relative to the distant stars.

🚀 Exploration

Humans have explored the Moon through several robotic and crewed missions.
