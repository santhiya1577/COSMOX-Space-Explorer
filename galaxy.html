🌌 What is a Galaxy?

A galaxy is a huge collection of stars, gas, dust and other objects held together by gravity.

🌀 Spiral Galaxy

Spiral galaxies have a bright central region surrounded by curved arms containing stars and gas.

⭐ Our Galaxy

Earth is located in the Milky Way, a large spiral galaxy containing billions of stars.

🔭 Galaxy Types

Galaxies are commonly grouped into spiral, elliptical, lenticular and irregular types.

🕳️ Black Holes

Many large galaxies are thought to contain a supermassive black hole near their center.
